About

Hello, I’m Sarah.

I’m a Lagos-based fashion illustrator and the founder of The SB Atelier. I draw fashion the way I see it: elegant silhouettes, flowing fabric and the small details that make a look feel special.

I’m currently growing my craft in digital fashion illustration, and this is where my work lives as it develops. I’d love to draw something for you.

Consultation process

How we work together

Whether it’s a custom illustration, a fashion portrait or a design idea, every piece starts with a conversation.

  1. 1

    Reach out

    Send a message on Instagram or by email with your idea and any reference photos.

  2. 2

    Consultation

    We talk through what you’d like drawn, the style, how you’ll use it and any date you have in mind.

  3. 3

    Proposal

    You receive the price and timeline in writing. Work begins only once you’re happy with them.

  4. 4

    Sketch

    You see a first sketch and can share your thoughts before the final illustration.

  5. 5

    Delivery

    Your finished illustration is sent to you as a high-quality digital file.

Reaching out is free and doesn’t commit you to anything.
